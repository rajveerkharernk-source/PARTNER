README · HOW TO CUSTOMISE

Customising Your Love Experience

1. Passcode & Security: Default code is 0214. Only its SHA-256 hash is stored in config.js. To change it, replace passcodeHash in config.js.

3. Editing Texts, Quiz & Music: Edit window.LOVE_CONFIG in config.js to update names, relationship start date, Heart of Words vocabulary, letter paragraphs, and playlist tracks.

4. Keyboard & Touch Shortcuts: Use ← / → or Space to navigate scenes, S to skip, R to replay the Midnight Rooftop sketch, and click any photo’s Continue → button in the lightbox to jump to its linked chapter.
